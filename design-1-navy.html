FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about our logistics services.

What is Over Dimension Cargo (ODC)?
ODC refers to cargo that exceeds standard vehicle dimensions in length, width, or weight. This includes industrial machinery, transformers, windmill blades, and construction equipment. Shipping ODC requires specialized permits, route surveys, escort vehicles, and custom loading solutions — exactly what we specialize in.
How much does ODC shipping cost from India?
Costs vary significantly based on cargo dimensions, weight, route complexity, and destination. A standard ODC shipment from Mumbai to UAE may range from ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000+. For an accurate quote, use our free quote form — we respond within 24 hours with a detailed, all-inclusive price.
What documents are needed for ODC shipping?
Typically you'll need: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading/airway bill, ODC permits from relevant authorities, cargo insurance certificate, IEC code (for imports/exports), and destination-specific documentation. We handle all of this on your behalf — you just provide us with your cargo details and business registration.
How do I track my shipment?
Every shipment we handle is tracked via real-time GPS. You'll receive a tracking number at booking and can monitor your cargo's location across all legs (road, ocean, air) through our tracking portal. We also send proactive WhatsApp and email alerts at every milestone.
Which countries do you serve?
We currently serve 30+ countries including the UAE, Singapore, Germany, USA, Australia, and all major Indian ports and cities. For air freight, we have partnerships with all major global carriers. For ocean freight, we work with all major shipping lines. If your destination isn't listed, ask us — we likely have a solution.
